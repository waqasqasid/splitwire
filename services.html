Common questions

How engagements actually work

How quickly can a technician be on-site?

Most requests are dispatched within a 1–2 day window. Emergency and after-hours dispatch is available 24/7 — call us directly for urgent jobs.

Are your technicians certified?

Yes. Every technician in our network is vetted and matched to jobs based on their certifications, skill set, and proximity to your site.

What happens after the job is done?

You receive a detailed digital service report covering the work performed, photos where relevant, and any follow-up recommendations. Then the ticket is closed.

Can you handle multi-site rollouts?

Yes. We scope, schedule and staff rollouts across multiple locations, with one point of contact coordinating the whole program.

How is pricing structured?

Single callouts are quoted per visit; projects and managed programs are scoped to the work. Describe the job and we'll send a quote.

Not sure which tier you need?

Describe the job — we'll scope the right technician and timeline.
